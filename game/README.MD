# Stay Inside

## What It Is

A short horror visual novel made in Ren'Py. You play as a student who gets stuck inside school after staying late with a group of friends. What starts as a normal evening quickly turns strange when someone begins knocking from outside.

The problem? The person outside knows things they shouldn't.

## Why I Made It

I wanted to make something short and psycholically creepy while experimenting more with Ren'Py. I was inspired a lot by "Class of '09", especially a game can have a casual, funny friend group before suddenly shifting into something darker.

## How I Made It

- Ren'Py

## What I Learnt And What I Struggled With

The biggest thing I learnt was definitely storytelling, making a story psychologically thrilling with a plan to make it a short horror game. I didn't want the scary parts to be abrupt so I tried to make the first chapter feel funny and normal and then slowly go into the scary part. Overall, this project was mainly an experimant in making a short horror story with Ren'Py.
